# 📜 Pytalon Assistant Code of Conduct

The Pytalon Assistant Community is made up of professionals, contributors, mentors, volunteers, and people from all over the world who work together across many areas of the project — including development, mentorship, teaching, collaboration, and community support.

Our diversity is one of our greatest strengths. At the same time, people with different backgrounds, experiences, and communication styles may occasionally encounter misunderstandings or disagreements.

This Code of Conduct exists to help prevent those situations and to ensure that everyone can participate in a welcoming, respectful, and productive environment.

It applies equally to everyone involved with the Pytalon Assistant Project, regardless of their role or level of involvement.

> **Please follow this Code of Conduct in the spirit in which it is intended: to help make the Pytalon Assistant community a place where everyone can learn, contribute, collaborate, and grow.**

---

## 🌎 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by the **Pytalon Assistant Project & Acubura**, including but not limited to:

- GitHub repositories
- Issues and pull requests
- GitHub Discussions
- Project events
- Community forums
- Chat platforms and other communication channels created or managed by the project team
- Any other spaces where the Pytalon Assistant community interacts

This Code of Conduct may also apply to behavior occurring outside of these spaces when that behavior has a significant impact on a person's ability to participate safely and respectfully within the Pytalon Assistant community.

---

# 🤝 Ground Rules

## 1. Be Friendly and Patient

Treat others with kindness and patience.

People have different levels of experience, communication styles, technical knowledge, and familiarity with the project. Take the time to explain things clearly and give others an opportunity to learn.

Everyone was a beginner at some point.

---

## 2. Be Welcoming

We strive to build a community where everyone feels welcome, valued, and supported.

We welcome people regardless of their:

- Race or ethnicity
- Culture or national origin
- Color
- Immigration status
- Social or economic background
- Educational background
- Sex
- Sexual orientation
- Gender identity or expression
- Age
- Body size
- Family or relationship status
- Political beliefs
- Religion or lack thereof
- Physical or mental ability
- Professional or technical experience

Everyone should have the opportunity to participate without fear of discrimination, harassment, or exclusion.

---

## 3. Be Considerate

Your work may be used by other people, just as you may depend on the work of others.

Consider the impact your decisions, comments, code, documentation, and actions may have on the community.

Remember that Pytalon Assistant is a community meant to serve people. Not everyone communicates in the same language or has the same cultural background.

When something is unclear, assume good intentions before assuming hostility.

---

## 4. Be Respectful

Disagreement is normal.

Technical discussions, design decisions, opinions, and approaches will not always align — and that's okay.

However, disagreement is never an excuse for:

- Personal attacks
- Insults
- Harassment
- Intimidation
- Hostile or threatening behavior
- Disrespectful communication

Focus on the problem rather than attacking the person.

A productive disagreement should help the community learn and improve, rather than make people feel unwelcome or threatened.

---

## 5. Choose Your Words Carefully

We are a community of professionals, contributors, learners, and volunteers. We expect everyone to communicate professionally and respectfully.

Do not insult, humiliate, demean, or deliberately provoke other participants.

Harassment, discrimination, and exclusionary behavior are not acceptable.

### This includes, but is not limited to:

- Violent threats or threatening language directed at another person
- Discriminatory jokes, comments, or language
- Racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic, or otherwise discriminatory insults
- Posting sexually explicit or excessively violent material
- Posting or threatening to post another person's private or personally identifying information (**doxxing**)
- Personal attacks or deliberate humiliation
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Persistent unwanted contact or harassment
- Encouraging or advocating any of the behavior listed above
- Repeatedly continuing behavior after someone has asked you to stop

> **If someone asks you to stop a particular behavior, respect their request and stop.**

---

## 6. Disagree Constructively

Technical and social disagreements are a normal part of any community.

When disagreements occur:

1. Listen to the other person's perspective.
2. Ask questions before making assumptions.
3. Focus on facts, ideas, and solutions rather than personalities.
4. Explain your reasoning clearly and respectfully.
5. Be willing to reconsider your position.
6. Accept that different approaches may sometimes be valid.
7. Learn from mistakes rather than assigning blame.

People come from different backgrounds and have different experiences. You may not always understand why someone holds a particular opinion, but that does not automatically make their perspective invalid.

> **The goal of a disagreement should be understanding and resolution — not winning.**

---

# 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated within the Pytalon Assistant community:

- Harassment or intimidation
- Discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Threats of violence
- Hate speech
- Doxxing or sharing private information
- Unwanted sexual attention
- Sexually explicit or excessively violent content
- Deliberate humiliation or bullying
- Repeated unwanted contact
- Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report
- Encouraging others to engage in prohibited behavior

This list is not exhaustive.

The project team may take appropriate action against other behavior that is reasonably considered harmful, abusive, threatening, or disruptive to the community.

# 📢 Reporting Guidelines

If you believe that someone is violating this Code of Conduct, please report the incident.

Reports can be submitted by email:

**📧 [acuburagamer100@gmail.com](mailto:acuburagamer100@gmail.com)**

When submitting a report, please provide as much of the following information as possible:

### What happened?

Provide a clear description of the incident, including relevant context.

### Where and when did it happen?

Include the relevant:

- GitHub issue
- Pull request
- Discussion
- Chat or communication channel
- Project event
- Date and approximate time

### Who was involved?

Provide usernames, profile links, or other relevant identifiers where possible.

### Is there supporting evidence?

Include any relevant:

- Screenshots
- Message links
- Issue or pull request links
- Other information that may help us understand what happened

Please do not intentionally alter or manipulate evidence.

---

# 🔒 Confidentiality

Reports will be reviewed by the project team and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

The project team will make a good-faith effort to protect the privacy of everyone involved and will not disclose the identity of a reporter without their consent except where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to address a serious safety or legal concern.

Reports will be evaluated fairly and based on the available information.

---

# 🛡️ No Retaliation

Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.

This includes retaliation against:

- Reporters
- Witnesses
- People cooperating with an investigation
- People who raise legitimate concerns about community safety

If you believe you are experiencing retaliation after making or supporting a report, please contact the project team using the reporting address above.

---

# ⚖️ Enforcement

The project team is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the circumstances and severity of a violation, possible actions may include:

- A private or public warning
- Requesting that the behavior stop
- Removal of inappropriate content
- Temporary restriction from project spaces
- Temporary suspension from participation
- Permanent removal from project spaces
- Other appropriate actions determined by the project team

Enforcement decisions will take the context, severity, frequency, and impact of the behavior into consideration.

The project team may also take immediate action when necessary to protect the safety and well-being of the community.

---

# ❤️ Our Commitment

The Pytalon Assistant community is built by people.

Every issue opened, pull request submitted, question answered, bug reported, tutorial written, and conversation started contributes to the project.

We ask everyone to help create an environment where people can:

- Learn without fear of embarrassment
- Ask questions without being judged
- Give and receive constructive feedback
- Share different perspectives
- Collaborate respectfully
- Make mistakes and learn from them
- Contribute regardless of experience level

**Be kind. Be respectful. Build together.**
